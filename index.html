Legend of Zelda Remake

A recreation of the original Legend of Zelda built in Unity, with a new mechanic of my own design added on top. The Wizzrobe Robe is an item Link picks up and equips as an alternate weapon. Using it sends Link dashing forward, damaging enemies he passes through and making him immune to damage while it lasts. It works as both a combat tool and a way to cross platforming gaps.

Features:

  • A dash that deals damage, grants invincibility frames, and has a short cooldown
  • Darknut enemies with shields that the dash can get past, leaving them stunned and facing the wrong way
  • A custom lava room with a wall that chases the player across it
  • Grid-based movement, room transitions, and enemies that only activate once they are on screen

My contributions:

  • UI elements
  • Bombs
  • The Wizzrobe Robe dash
  • Link's movement
  • Room transitions
  • The collection system
  • The custom level's layout and design

Technology used:

  • Unity
  • C#
  • GIMP (pixel art)
